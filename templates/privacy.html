Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the app, we also store the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide the core functionality of Spendly: displaying your expenses, generating category breakdowns and summaries, and keeping your account secure. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your data will be removed within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third parties. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) to operate the app, and those providers only process data as needed to deliver their service to us.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
